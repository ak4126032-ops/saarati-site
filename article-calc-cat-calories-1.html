How this is calculated

The starting point is a cat's resting energy requirement (RER) — the calories it would burn doing nothing at all — worked out from body weight using the formula 70 × (weight in kg) 0.75. From there, a multiplier is applied based on life stage, spay/neuter status, and goal:

  • Kittens under 4 months need roughly 3x their RER to fuel rapid growth.
  • Kittens 4–12 months need about 2x RER as growth slows.
  • Neutered adult cats maintain weight around 1.2x RER; intact adults run a bit higher, near 1.4x.
  • Weight loss targets sit closer to 1x RER, with the plan re-checked as weight changes.
  • Weight gain for underweight cats runs higher, around 1.4–1.6x RER.
  • Senior cats usually need slightly less than a younger adult at the same weight.
